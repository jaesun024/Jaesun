Kyrie Irving

About Kyrie Irving

Kyrie Irving is a professional basketball player known for his amazing ball-handling skills, shooting, and finishing. He is one of the most talented basketball players of his generation.

Kyrie was born on March 23, 1992. He entered the NBA in 2011 after being selected as the first overall pick by the Cleveland Cavaliers.

His Career

Kyrie has played for the Cleveland Cavaliers, Boston Celtics, Brooklyn Nets, and Dallas Mavericks. Throughout his career, he has become known for his quick dribbling and difficult shots.

In 2016, Kyrie helped the Cleveland Cavaliers win the NBA Championship. He also made an important three-point shot during Game 7 of the NBA Finals.

Quick Facts

Position: Point Guard

Height: 6 feet 2 inches

Born: March 23, 1992

NBA Draft: 1st Overall in 2011

NBA Championship: 2016

Why I Like Kyrie

I like Kyrie Irving because of his incredible basketball skills. His dribbling, shooting, and ability to score make him exciting to watch. He shows that practice and hard work can help a player improve and become successful.
