Why Nutrition Matters

Good food gives you energy, keeps you healthy, and helps you think clearly.



Food Groups

Fruits & Vegetables - Eat 5 servings daily for vitamins and fiber.

Grains & Proteins - Whole grains, meat, fish, and beans for energy.

Dairy & Fats - Milk, cheese, nuts for strong bones and heart.



Quick Tips

Drink 8 glasses of water daily

Fill half your plate with fruits and veggies

Choose whole grains over refined ones

Don't skip breakfast

Limit processed food and sugar



Start Today

Small changes make a big difference.
